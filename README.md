# SleepyRide

A Flutter application to help you sleep peacefully during your commute and wake you up when you reach your destination.

## Features

*   **Set Destination:** Easily search and set your destination.
*   **Wake-up Song:** Choose a song to wake you up.
*   **Location Tracking:** The app tracks your location in the background.
*   **Automatic Wake-up:** The app automatically plays the wake-up song and vibrates when you are near your destination.
*   **Recent Destinations:** Quickly access your recent destinations.

## How to Use

1.  **Set Destination:** Tap on "Set Destination" and search for your desired location.
2.  **Select Wake-up Song:** Tap on "Select Wake-up Song" and choose a song from your device.
3.  **Start Ride:** Tap on "Start Ride" to begin your journey.
4.  **Sleep Peacefully:** The app will run in the background and track your location.
5.  **Wake-up:** The app will wake you up when you are 200 meters away from your destination.
